Çini – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 7 November 2026

Çini ("the app", "the game") is developed by Messo Technology (Mustafa Emir Koçyiğit) ("we"). This policy explains what data the app processes and why. The app has no accounts or sign-up, and it is not directed at children under 13.

Data we and our partners process

  • Game progress and settings (levels, coins, preferences): stored only on your device. We do not receive it.
  • Advertising data: the app shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and device/app information to serve and measure ads, and to personalise them if you consent where required.
  • Usage analytics: Firebase Analytics records events such as levels started or completed, together with device and app information and an app-instance identifier, to help us improve the game.
  • Crash reports: Firebase Crashlytics collects crash logs, device model, OS version and an app-instance identifier to help us fix bugs.
  • Remote configuration: Firebase Remote Config fetches game settings from Google servers; this involves standard request data such as IP address.
  • Purchases: in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details; we only receive confirmation that a purchase was made.
  • Notifications: reminders are scheduled locally on your device after you grant permission. You can turn them off in the app or in system settings.

Purposes

Providing and improving the game, showing and measuring ads, fixing crashes, preventing abuse and processing purchases.

Third parties

  • Google AdMob / Google Ads: policies.google.com/privacy · how Google uses data
  • Firebase (Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • Google Play Billing: policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell your personal data.

Consent and your choices

Where required (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app shows a consent form for ads. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options inside the app. You can also reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads).

Your rights

Depending on where you live (including under the GDPR and Turkey's KVKK) you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. Because we hold no account data, most data is held by Google as described above; you can contact us and we will help with your request. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Retention

Local game data stays on your device until you delete the app. Analytics and crash data are retained by Firebase according to Google's standard retention periods (analytics event data is typically kept for up to 14 months).

Children

The app is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us.

International transfers

Google may process data in countries outside your own, using safeguards such as standard contractual clauses.

Changes

We may update this policy; the effective date above will change when we do.
